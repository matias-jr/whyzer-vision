Scope

Platform vs point of view

Orbitshift is broad. It describes itself as an enterprise sales platform with 50+ interconnected AI agents, organized into modules:

prospectOS for finding high-intent accounts and engaging prospects
accountOS for account intelligence and growing existing accounts
rfpOS for responding to RFPs and competitive bids
agentOS for agents across the sales cycle

It plugs into Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics, SharePoint, Google Drive, Teams, Slack, Outreach, and Marketo. Its own team of forward-deployed sales experts builds custom agents for customers. And it's especially strong in IT and business services, with customers like Infosys, Wipro, Tech Mahindra, and HCLTech.

Whyzer is narrow on purpose.

It does one job: read an account's financial story and turn it into a sourced point of view a senior executive will respond to. Then it gives you the emails, conversation starters, and executive deck to deliver it.

The question is which problem you're solving. Running an entire enterprise sales motion on one platform? Or winning the executive conversation on the accounts that decide your year?

About Orbitshift

What is Orbitshift?+

Orbitshift is an AI-native enterprise sales platform with 50+ AI agents across prospecting (prospectOS), account intelligence (accountOS), RFP response (rfpOS), and custom agents (agentOS). It integrates with Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics, and other enterprise systems.

How much does Orbitshift cost?+

As of October 2026, Orbitshift offers a free trial and doesn't publish pricing. Contact Orbitshift for team and enterprise pricing.

What's the difference between Orbitshift and Whyzer?+

Orbitshift is a broad platform for enterprise sales teams, covering prospecting through proposals. Whyzer is a focused tool for the individual seller, built to turn an account's financial story into an executive point of view, with Jamal Reimer's selling method and live coaching on Elite.
