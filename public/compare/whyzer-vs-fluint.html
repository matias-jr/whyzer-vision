What changed

First, a note on what Fluint is today

If you knew Fluint a year or two ago, you probably knew it for buyer enablement: helping sellers build business cases and champion materials for deals already in motion.

Fluint now describes itself as a revenue engineering platform. It's built for RevOps teams, and it has three parts:

Loop, a context layer. It reads your GTM data (Salesforce, HubSpot, Gong, Outreach, email, your data warehouse) and serves pre-enriched deal context to any AI agent over MCP.
Private models trained on your own deal outcomes, so you can see which activities actually correlate with closed-won in your business.
A managed AI sales assistant, configured for your team and running in Slack, email, and the browser.

That shift matters for this comparison, because Fluint's intelligence comes from inside your company, and the first executive meeting is won with intelligence from outside it.

Where each tool earns its keep

The deal stage question

Think about where each tool earns its keep.

Before there's a deal

Outside-in intelligence

You're trying to get a senior executive at a target account to give you 30 minutes. They don't know you. Your CRM has nothing useful on them. What you need is outside-in intelligence: what this company is under pressure to fix, in their own numbers, and a reason to talk to you about it. That's Whyzer's job.

Once the deal is in motion

Inside-out context

Now there's activity in your systems. Calls, emails, stakeholder changes, stage movement. The question becomes which actions move deals like this one to close, based on everything your team has won and lost before. That's where Fluint is built to help.

Using Fluint and Whyzer together

FAQ

About Fluint

What is Fluint?+

Fluint is a revenue engineering platform for RevOps teams. It turns internal GTM data from systems like Salesforce, HubSpot, and Gong into pre-enriched context for AI agents, trains private models on a company's deal outcomes, and offers a managed AI sales assistant.

How much does Fluint cost?+

As of October 2026, Fluint's Build plan starts at $600/month billed monthly, Scale starts at $1,590/month with a managed assistant at $99 per seat (10-seat minimum), and Enterprise starts at $4K/month. There's a 14-day free trial. Check fluint.io/pricing for current details.

Does Fluint use financial data like 10-Ks and earnings calls?+

Fluint's platform is built on your internal GTM data: CRM, calls, email, and warehouse data. Its public materials don't describe 10-K or earnings call analysis.

Can I use Fluint and Whyzer together?+

Yes. They work on different data at different stages. Whyzer builds the outside-in argument that earns the first executive meeting. Fluint helps once the deal is in your systems.

Which is better for getting a first meeting with an executive?+

Whyzer. It's built specifically to turn an account's financial story into a point of view a senior executive will respond to. See the full comparison of AI account intelligence tools.
