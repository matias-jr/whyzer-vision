Signals vs arguments

Finding the signal vs making the argument

Here's what these two tools have in common: both read earnings calls and financial filings. Both are self-serve. Both are priced for an individual seller. The difference is what they hand you at the end.

Salesmotion

Built to tell you something happened

It watches your accounts around the clock. A new CFO. A hiring surge. Guidance cut on an earnings call. It flags the change, gives you a research brief, finds the right contacts with verified emails and direct dials, and drafts outreach tied to the event.

Whyzer

Built to tell you why it matters to one executive

It reads the same kinds of sources and asks a different question. What is this company under pressure to fix, in its own numbers? Which executive owns that pressure? What would change their mind? Then it builds the point of view, the email, and the deck around the answer.

A signal is a fact. An argument is why that fact should keep the CFO up at night.

Most sellers don't have a signal shortage. They have more alerts than they can act on. What wins the meeting is the reason the executive should care, said better than the eleven other sellers who saw the same alert.

Quick definition

What is a sales motion?

If you searched "sales motion," here's the short version. A sales motion is the repeatable way a company finds, engages, and closes customers: who you target, how you reach them, and the steps from first contact to signature. Enterprise sales motions tend to be long, multi-stakeholder, and executive-driven.

If you're thinking about how deals get started inside large accounts, the rest of this page compares two tools built to help with that first step.

FAQ

About Salesmotion

What is Salesmotion?+

Salesmotion is an account intelligence platform for B2B sales teams. AI agents monitor accounts for buying signals, build research briefs from sources including earnings calls, filings, job posts, and news, supply verified contacts, and draft outreach.

How much does Salesmotion cost?+

As of October 2026, Salesmotion's Individual plan is $85/month, billed monthly. Team and enterprise plans are custom priced. Check salesmotion.io/pricing for current details.

Does Salesmotion use earnings calls?+

Yes. Salesmotion's research sources include earnings call transcripts and SEC filings, along with job posts, slides, and news.

What's the difference between Salesmotion and Whyzer?+

Salesmotion focuses on finding signals, contacts, and drafting outreach across many accounts. Whyzer focuses on turning a company's financial story into a sourced executive point of view, using Jamal Reimer's selling method, with live coaching on Elite.
